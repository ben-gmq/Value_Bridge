# Value Bridge — target UI for proposals (2026-10-16)

2880 × 1800 PNG (a 1440 × 900 screen at 2× for sharp slides), dark theme. All names, figures, people and
organisations are illustrative (the "2027 Core Systems Renewal" showcase project) — no client data.

**Source:** 01–10 are screenshots of the running app (built today). 11–12 are mockups of designed features that
are **not built yet** (source in `docs/mockups/src/screens/11_*`, `12_*`; caption them as such in a proposal).

| # | File | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01_scope-dashboard.png` | Scope dashboard: requirements in the signed-off baseline, coverage by functional requirement, effort in scope and changes since the last baseline, by business area. |
| 02 | `02_function-chart-requirement.png` | Business function chart: every process step carries its business requirement, business logic and expected output, plus who performs it and what data it uses. |
| 03 | `03_process-readiness.png` | Readiness at a glance: each process shows a ready / not-ready light, and the gaps that would block a baseline freeze are listed by step. |
| 04 | `04_data-flow-diagram.png` | Data flow diagram generated from the processes, the data they read and write and who performs them — by position, with outside parties and triggers. |
| 05 | `05_data-entity.png` | Data entity with its fields and keys, and every requirement that creates, reads or updates it. |
| 06 | `06_solution.png` | Solution view: the requirements it answers, the functional requirements under it, the system that delivers it and the project's solution mix. |
| 07 | `07_function-requirement.png` | Functional requirement priced from the rate card by type and complexity, with traceability back to requirements and processes. |
| 08 | `08_effort-summary.png` | Effort summary: man-days derived from the rate card, never typed — by functional-requirement type and by solution. |
| 09 | `09_baseline-comparison.png` | Baselines: freeze the agreed scope, record the client's sign-off, and see every change since — priced in man-days — with a consistency check before the next freeze. |
| 10 | `10_time-and-fte.png` | Time & FTE: annual hours per business area today beside the future process, and the hours and FTE the change saves. |
| 11 | `11_scope-dashboard-target.png` | *Designed, not yet built.* The full scope dashboard: adds processes not ready, open freeze warnings, entities without fields, effort by solution and a business-area filter. |
| 12 | `12_structured-requirement-target.png` | *Designed, not yet built.* Structured requirements: numbered requirement lines with kind and priority, the exact data attributes used, and data that must move between systems — each covered by an interface or migration requirement. |
